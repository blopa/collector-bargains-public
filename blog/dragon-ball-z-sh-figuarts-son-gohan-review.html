Dragon Ball Z S.H. Figuarts Son Gohan Review

Bandai Tamashii Nations' Dragon Ball S.H. Figuarts line has long been a favorite among collectors for its impressive detail and articulation. This Son Gohan figure is no exception, capturing the intensity of his Super Saiyan form during the Cell Saga. The sculpt is incredibly dynamic, showcasing Gohan's powerful physique and determined expression. The paint application is precise and clean, with vibrant colors that truly pop. Even small details, like the texture of his hair and gi, are faithfully recreated. This makes it a must-have for any serious DBZ collector.

One of the most impressive aspects of this figure is its articulation. The numerous points of articulation allow for a wide range of poses, capturing Gohan's signature fighting stances and powerful attacks. The joints are tight and secure, ensuring that the figure holds its pose without sagging. This is crucial for recreating iconic scenes from the anime. The figure also includes several interchangeable hands and facial expressions, allowing for even greater customization and display options. These extra pieces further enhance the overall value and playability of the figure.

The accessories included with the figure are also noteworthy. While not overly abundant, they are thoughtfully chosen to enhance the display options. The inclusion of a Kamehameha effect part adds a dynamic element to any pose. While some collectors may desire more accessories, such as additional hands or special energy blasts, the selection provided effectively elevates the figure's presentation. This thoughtful curation makes this figure feel substantial and special.

In conclusion, the Dragon Ball Z S.H. Figuarts Super Saiyan Son Gohan figure is a fantastic addition to any collection. The exceptional sculpt, articulation, and paint job combine to create a truly stunning piece. While the price point may be higher than some other figures, the quality and attention to detail make it a worthwhile investment for discerning collectors. If you're a fan of Dragon Ball Z and appreciate high-quality action figures, this Gohan figure is a must-buy.
